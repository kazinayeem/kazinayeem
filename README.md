## 📖 About Me

<img align="right" src="https://bornosoftnr.com/logo.png" alt="Bornosoft" width="140" />

I'm a **Software Engineering student** with an obsession for **backend architecture**, **cloud infrastructure**, and **artificial intelligence**. Currently based in **Bangladesh**, I spend my days designing scalable distributed systems, architecting cloud-native infrastructure on AWS, and building AI-powered SaaS platforms.

I believe the best software is built at the intersection of **robust engineering** and **intelligent automation**. Every line of code I write is driven by a singular mission — to build enterprise-grade solutions that compete on a global stage, right from Dhaka.

As the **Founder of Bornosoft**, I'm building more than just software. I'm building a company that will prove that world-class technology products can — and will — emerge from Bangladesh. My vision spans **SaaS platforms**, **AI agents**, **cloud infrastructure**, and **enterprise automation**.

**My current engineering work involves:**
- Architecting **microservices** with **NestJS** and **Node.js**
- Deploying **cloud-native** infrastructure on **AWS**
- Building **CI/CD pipelines** with **GitHub Actions** and **Docker**
- Developing **AI agents** using **LangChain**, **OpenAI**, and **vector databases**
- Designing **multi-tenant SaaS architectures** at scale

I'm also deeply committed to research. My interests span **LLMs**, **distributed systems**, **MLOps**, and **AI infrastructure**. I aim to publish research and pursue an **MSc/PhD** abroad, contributing to the academic community while continuing to build products that matter.
